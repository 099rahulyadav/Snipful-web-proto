Questions

Before you choose.

Everything you need to understand the two Snipful plans.

Can I use Snipful for free indefinitely?

Yes. The Free plan includes the core product and stays available as long as your library remains within its Collection, Stash and Snippet limits.

What happens when I reach a Free plan limit?

Your existing library remains intact. Upgrade to Pro when you need more Collections or want to keep unlimited Stashes and Snippets.

Are private snippets included in Free?

Yes. Public, unlisted and private visibility options are part of the core sharing experience on both plans.

Which features require Pro?

AI-powered search, AI summaries, advanced filtering, analytics, version history and rollback are available on Pro.

Can I import and export my snippets?

Yes. Import and export are core capabilities available on both Free and Pro.
